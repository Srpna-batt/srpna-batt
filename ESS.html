Safety Usage Guide

• Do not disassemble, crush, incinerate or heat the battery.

• Use batteries from the same manufacturer; do not mix different brands or models.

• Do not immerse the battery in water or allow it to get wet.

• Do not short-circuit, overcharge or over-discharge the battery.

• Do not install or store near heat sources or open flames.

• Do not puncture, impact, throw or step on the battery.

• Stop using the battery immediately if it emits odor, heats up, deforms or shows any abnormality.

• In case of fire, cut off high/low voltage circuits and use dry powder, sand or sufficient water to extinguish.
