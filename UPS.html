Safety Usage Guide

• Do not disassemble, crush, incinerate, heat or put the battery into fire.

• Use batteries from the same manufacturer; mixing different brands may cause fire or explosion.

• Do not immerse the battery in water or allow it to get wet.

• Do not short-circuit, overcharge or over-discharge the battery.

• Do not install, use or store the battery near heat sources or heaters.

• Do not puncture the case; avoid impact, throwing, stepping on or rolling the battery.

• If the battery emits odor, heat, deforms or shows any abnormality, stop use immediately.

• In case of fire, cut off high/low voltage circuits first; use dry powder or sand. If water is used, use sufficient water to flood for a long time.
