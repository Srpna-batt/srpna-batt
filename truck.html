Safety Warnings & Precautions

• Prohibit short circuit — never bridge the positive and negative poles.

• Prohibit disassembly — non-professionals must not open the pack.

• Fire prevention — install on a fireproof surface, away from flammable materials.

• Protective measures — wear insulated gloves and goggles during operation.

• Charging — must use the matching charger.

• Temperature — strictly comply with the operating temperature range.

• Maintenance — inspect and service regularly as required.

• Environment — avoid installation in humid or dusty locations.
