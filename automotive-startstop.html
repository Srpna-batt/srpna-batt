SRPNA+ Sodium-ion Start-Stop Battery Series

High Performance Sodium-ion Batteries for Automotive Start-Stop Systems

Product Overview

The SRPNA+ Sodium-ion Start-Stop Battery Series is a new generation of automotive power batteries designed for modern vehicle start-stop systems. Featuring advanced NFPP sodium-ion cell technology, these batteries deliver exceptional cycle life, extreme temperature performance, and high CCA output, making them an ideal replacement for traditional lead-acid batteries. With a standard 12V rated voltage, the series offers 5 models with varying power and size specifications to fit a wide range of passenger and commercial vehicles.

Core Product Features

Feature 1

Ultra-long Cycle Life: ≥6000 cycles, far exceeding traditional lead-acid batteries, ensuring long-term reliable performance for start-stop applications

Feature 2

Excellent Extreme Temperature Performance: Discharge range from -50°C to 80°C, stable power output in extreme cold and hot weather conditions

Feature 3

High CCA Starting Performance: Up to 1000A CCA, providing strong and reliable starting power for various passenger and commercial vehicles

Feature 4

Ultra-low Internal Resistance: ≤5mΩ, supporting high power output and fast charging capability, meeting the high power demand of start-stop systems

Feature 5

High Safety & Reliability: Stable NFPP sodium-ion cell chemistry, flame-retardant PC+ABS housing, no risk of thermal runaway under normal use

Feature 6

Low Self-Discharge: ≤2% per month, maintaining sufficient power for vehicle starting even after long-term parking
